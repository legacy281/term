Privacy Policy for MyAirportMap

Last updated: May 04, 2026

MyAirportMap ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed by MyAirportMap.

1. Information We Collect

- Account Information: When you register, we collect your email, username, and avatar.

- Flight Data: We collect information about airport visits you log, including dates, callsigns, and notes.

- Usage Data: We may collect information on how the app is accessed and used.

- Purchase Data: For premium subscriptions, transaction processing is handled by Apple and Google. We do not store your credit card information.

2. How We Use Information

- To provide and maintain our Service (Pilot's Lounge, Map syncing).

- To manage your account and subscriptions.

- To improve our application features.

3. Data Sharing

- Pilot's Lounge: Your username, avatar, and total airport count are visible to other users if you opt-in.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: contact@bluenexus.us
